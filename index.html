I'm Seif — a Cairo-based content and digital marketing enthusiast with a research background. I write, script, and build content that moves people, and I bring a research habit of testing ideas against evidence rather than guessing.

About

My background is in sustainable design engineering, and it shapes how I approach marketing: I treat every piece of content as a small experiment — start with a clear idea of the audience, write toward one outcome, and check whether it actually landed.

That habit was built through research work, and it now shows up in how I plan content: leading student initiatives, mentoring, running communications for an international student network, and — most recently — studying content marketing directly through a DEPI course, where I'm building out a full storytelling and video-script campaign.

What I bring to a marketing team: a genuine curiosity about people, comfort working across cultures and organizations, and the discipline to turn a rough idea into a structured piece of content — whether that's a TikTok script, a campaign narrative, or a research-backed message.
